Hi, I'm Ben. I'm a materials scientist who became a forecaster, and I like questions whose best answer is a probability.

I forecast professionally with the RAND Forecasting Initiative and Metaculus, where I won the Summer 2026 Metaculus Cup, and I'm a member of Samotsvety. I write about science, AI, and claims that don't hold up on my blog, The BS Detector.

Before that, I spent five years at Northwestern on a PhD in materials science, building sorbents that pull heavy metals out of water and carbon dioxide out of the air, with a side project on what tea leaves do to the water you brew them in.

I also organize gatherings for people who think about the future, like Manifest x DC and The Overhang. Otherwise I'm constructing crosswords, collecting ancient coins, or playing basketball at my county gym.
